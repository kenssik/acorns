**MOD "ACORNS" 2.0 — for Minecraft 1.21.1 (Fabric)**

    The giant acorn is now neat, exactly 8x8x8, round. Inside there's a hollow
    chamber with a chest (and a couple of rotten acorns...). Found in all forests.

    Acorns in all biomes: under the leaves of any trees (variety depends on climate:
    snowy, golden, dark, regular), glowing ones — in caves of all biomes.

    Rotten acorns lie on the ground everywhere. Break or eat one — COCKROACHES
    come running out and bite. With a 10% chance, instead of a cockroach out runs
    a RUNNING PHONE: wearing sneakers, with a frightened face, ringing and running away.
    Catch it — you get a smartphone with silly notifications (right-click).

    SLIPPER: kills a cockroach in one hit, knocks the rest away with a
    "SMACK" sound. Craft: 2 leather + 1 wool (shapeless).

    Structures: abandoned castles (towers, gates, throne room, well),
    abandoned forester's huts, ruined watchtowers. They contain chests.
